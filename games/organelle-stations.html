Organelle Structure Function Category Present In

Rough ER Network of tubes with ribosomes attached Transports proteins; protein synthesis Transport/Synthesis Plant & Animal

Lysosomes Small, round sacs with enzymes Breaks down waste and old cell parts Storage/Recycling Animal (rare in plants)
Vacuoles Large sac (plants); small (animals) Stores water, nutrients, waste Storage Plant & Animal
Chloroplasts Oval, green; contains chlorophyll Captures light energy for photosynthesis Energy Plant only
Cell Membrane Thin, flexible layer around cell Controls what enters and exits the cell Transport Plant & Animal
Cell Wall Rigid outer layer Provides support and protection Support Plant only
