Organelle Structure Function Category Present in
Nucleus Large, membrane-bound; contains DNA Controls cell activities; stores genetic info Synthesis Plant & Animal
Mitochondria Oval, double membrane; cristae folds Produces ATP energy (respiration) Energy Plant & Animal
Ribosomes Tiny dots; free or on ER Makes proteins Synthesis Plant & Animal
Rough ER Network of tubes + ribosomes Transports proteins; protein synthesis Transport Plant & Animal
Smooth ER Network of tubes, no ribosomes Makes lipids; detoxifies Transport Plant & Animal
Golgi Apparatus Stack of flattened sacs Modifies, sorts, packages proteins Transport Plant & Animal
Lysosomes Small sacs with enzymes Breaks down waste and old cell parts Storage Animal (rare in plants)
Vacuoles Large sac (plants); small (animals) Stores water, nutrients, waste Storage Plant & Animal
Chloroplasts Oval, green; contains chlorophyll Captures light energy (photosynthesis) Energy Plant only
Cell Membrane Thin, flexible layer around cell Controls what enters/exits the cell Transport Plant & Animal
Cell Wall Rigid outer layer Provides support and protection Support Plant only

Organelle Stations Rotation (6 stations)

Station 1

Nucleus & Ribosomes

Nucleus with pores; ribosomes as tiny dots.

Why does the nucleus have pores?
DNA stretched = 2 meters!
Station 2

Mitochondria & Chloroplasts

Cristae folds; thylakoids and grana.

Why do muscle cells have more mitochondria?
Mitochondria have their own DNA!
Station 3

Rough & Smooth ER

Rough ER with ribosomes; smooth ER without.

Why is rough ER "rough"?
Liver cells have lots of smooth ER.
Station 4

Golgi & Vesicles

Stacked sacs; vesicles budding off.

How is the Golgi like a post office?
Discovered by Camillo Golgi in 1898.
Station 5

Lysosomes & Vacuoles

Enzyme sacs; large central vacuole in plants.

Why do plant cells have a large vacuole?
Lysosomes = "suicide sacs"
Station 6

Cell Membrane & Wall

Phospholipid bilayer; rigid cell wall.

Why is the membrane "selectively permeable"?
10,000 membranes = paper thickness.

Exit Ticket: Which organelle surprised you most & why?

Common confusions: Rough vs smooth ER · Golgi vs ER · Lysosome vs vacuole · Mitochondria vs chloroplast
